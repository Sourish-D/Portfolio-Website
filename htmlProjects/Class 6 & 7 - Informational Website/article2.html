Councillor's comments on Chow's age at 1st council meeting as Toronto mayor spark backlash

Abby O'Brien
CTV News Toronto Multi-Platform Writer



Comments made by a Toronto city councillor about newly-elected mayor Olivia Chow's age during her first council meeting since assuming the role have sparked backlash. The comments were made by Eglinton - Lawrence Councillor Mike Colle at Wednesday's council meeting as he spoke on a group of asylum seekers sleeping on the streets outside of a Toronto shelter over recent weeks.
